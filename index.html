Gendered Versus Genderless Pronouns: An Empirical Study on How NLP Models Exhibit Bias with Gender Embedded Contexts

Ethan Cao, Jacquelyn Xu, Alex Schwartz

University of Virginia · CS 2501 Introduction to Research, advised by Prof. Brianna Morrison · December 2025

We apply Perturbation Sensitivity Analysis to Reddit comments from the RtGender dataset to test whether toxicity and sentiment models label text differently in languages with gendered pronouns (English, Dutch) than in languages with only gender-neutral pronouns (Finnish, Turkish).
